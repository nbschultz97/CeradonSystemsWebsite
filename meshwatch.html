meshwatch

A Meshtastic client for Garmin watches. The watch talks to your radio over Bluetooth and shows you the mesh on your wrist — who is up, how much battery they have, when they were last heard, and the message traffic. Phone-free and internet-free.

  • ▹ Node roster with battery level and last-heard timers.
  • ▹ Message log, plus a canned-message picker for gloves-on replies.
  • ▹ Runs on the tactix and fenix line — ten devices, from fenix 6X Pro to fenix 8.
  • ▹ Your phone stays in the pack. Nothing touches the internet.

How it works — and why it needs a bridge

A Garmin watch cannot read a Meshtastic node directly. Connect IQ caps every Bluetooth read at about 20 bytes with no way to negotiate a larger one, and a Meshtastic packet spends most of that on framing — so a watch talking straight to a node recovers node numbers and nothing else. No names, no message text.

So a small ESP32 bridge does the reading. It connects to a stock, unmodified Meshtastic node at full packet size, then re-serves the traffic to the watch in frames that fit. The node still does all the radio work; the bridge never touches LoRa.

Status: not shipping yet

The watch app is complete and verified across ten devices. The bridge firmware compiles but has not yet been run on hardware. We are not taking money for it until it has been proven on a real node.
