Your aircraft,
part by part.

Choose a frame, motors, props, battery and electronics. Save aircraft for repeatable flights, with the current catalog and each build's source inventory kept in view.

Bring your controls

Use keyboard, gamepad or a compatible USB-joystick RC transmitter. Calibrate flight axes, adjust inversion and rates, and save the setup. Device support varies; map-action binding is not a full action-binding suite.

Choose the ground and conditions

Preview a city or coordinate search, adjust the launch pin, then continue. Practice with configured time, weather and wind, an FPV view or a local line-of-sight camera. Configured weather is not a live weather service.

Change the light.
Change the conditions.

Compare configured morning, noon, afternoon and rain. Time of day and weather change the simulated environment; these settings are not observations from a live weather service.

Make a scenario
you can return to.

Choose a launch point, place scene objects and set objectives in Mission Builder. Save, import and export mission files to repeat a scenario with different aircraft and settings.

Review the loading brief

See the launch location, objective and route before entering the scene.

Strike scenarios.
An ISR view.

FPV approach and ISR aftermath from a scripted in-game capture. The separate camera shows the scene after the effect; the outcome is not confirmed.

A shared flight,
still in testing.

Host or join a LAN lobby, select an aircraft and ready up before launch. Discovery, separate spawns, armed input and reciprocal movement passed a two-process test on one PC.

What changed
in v0.42.11.

Catalog, data, map and Work Bench updates are now in the public release. The published notes separate shipped changes from unfinished work.

Your catalogs,
without changing the base

Create, import, export, copy, rename and switch custom catalogs. Clone the read-only bundled catalog to make your own, then edit a validated JSON draft.

Work Bench distinguishes the current inventory from a saved aircraft's source inventory and reports missing source catalogs. A per-part form editor is not included.

Local data,
with explicit placement

Organize named local data collections, place supported image overlays and run an explicit Cesium connection check. The intended public terrain configuration is included.

Raw point clouds, GIS files and archives still need conversion and suitable placement metadata. They are not ready-to-fly terrain imports.

Find a place.
Keep your bearings.

City or coordinate search previews a movable pin before launch. Major-city labels and a coarse offline overview aid orientation. Free Flight also has a C2 map with place labels and a compass.

This is not online street-address or point-of-interest search, live weather or an offline worldwide street map. The live mission C2 missing-marker regression remains open.

A clearer aircraft setup

Click-only aircraft selection, selected-build predictions, stock starters, targeted ten-inch prop compatibility and thumbnail-residency changes make setup easier to follow.

Frame-grounded supports, stack spacers and selected board mounts carry representative, unverified or conflict labels. Exact mounts, straps, wiring and artwork still need work.

Targeted fixes also cover per-aircraft audio teardown, terrain requests, Cesium lifecycle handling and Settings readability. Read the detailed changelog.

Know the limits
before you download.

KESTREL is alpha software, not a finished roadmap. The published package passed a fresh cook, 70 native tests, 218 launcher tests and targeted packaged checks.

That does not certify every controller, display mode, aircraft combination or multiplayer session. Screenshots show the current game, including its remaining UI and art defects.

Not included yet

  • Full action rebinding across keyboard, gamepad and radio.
  • A Work Bench OSD editor or rotating multiplayer aircraft cards.
  • Live weather or online address and point-of-interest lookup.
  • Exact models and complete mechanical-fit validation for every catalog item.

Still being verified or corrected

Live mission C2 may omit launch and objective markers or the connecting route. Some UI still overlaps or clips. Battery artwork, carbon appearance, part mounting, straps and wiring need further work.

Physical USB-radio and display-mode checks, two-PC LAN, mission networking and host loss, calibrated camera/prop/flight-feel comparisons, terrain stress and actual-install updater/rollback checks remain open.
