An aircraft you configure.
A flight you can repeat.

The core simulator brings aircraft configuration, controls, location selection and session review into one practice loop.

Start at the workbench

Choose an airframe, motors, propellers, battery and other catalog components. Their specifications inform simulated mass, thrust and power use. Save builds for repeatable comparisons.

Predictions are simulator estimates, not measured flight performance or proof that real parts fit together.

Bring your controls

Use keyboard, gamepad or a compatible USB-joystick RC transmitter. Calibrate flight axes, adjust inversion and rates, and save the setup. Supported Betaflight settings can inform the simulated control feel.

Device support varies. Map-button capture in the candidate is not full action rebinding.

Choose your ground

Select a location using coordinates or a map. Streamed photoreal terrain is available where the provider has coverage; a generated environment supports the basic practice loop.

Fly the conditions

Location and time shape the lighting. Practice daylight and low-light views, with simulated wind, battery use, link degradation and damage cues.

Read the flight

An FPV flight-controller-style OSD presents flight information. A local line-of-sight camera offers another view. These are simulator views, not a certification of real camera optics.

Compare your runs

Structured objectives and local session records help compare completion, time and score. Repeat a scenario with the same aircraft and settings to see what changed.

Make the practice
your own.

Build a scenario around a route, a position hold, an observation dwell or a landing objective. Place scene objects, choose a launch point, then save and fly.

Mission file import and export support repeatable scenarios. Terrain loading and placement still need to be checked in the selected environment.

More control over
what you bring into the sim.

These additions are not in the public v0.42.9 evaluation. They remain subject to fresh packaged validation and are not a promise that every device, dataset or aircraft combination is supported.

Your catalogs,
without changing the base

Create, import, export, copy, rename and switch custom catalogs. Clone the read-only bundled catalog to make your own. Edit a validated JSON draft; a per-part form editor is not included.

Work Bench distinguishes the current inventory from a saved aircraft's source inventory. Missing source catalogs are reported rather than silently substituting components. Explicit receiver-supplied antenna metadata keeps ownership and included mass clear.

Local data,
with explicit placement

Organize local data collections, add supported image overlays with placement sidecars, and run an explicit Cesium connection check.

Raw point clouds, GIS files and archives are not ready-to-fly terrain imports. They need conversion and suitable placement metadata first.

Find a place.
Keep your bearings.

Preview city or coordinate searches, move the launch pin, then explicitly choose where to fly or build. A coarse offline overview supports orientation.

The in-flight C2 map adds available city labels and a compass. City coverage is not neighborhood, street-address or online point-of-interest search; offline overview data is not detailed terrain.

A clearer Work Bench

Click-only saved-aircraft selection, predictions for the selected build, starter aircraft and targeted prop compatibility checks make aircraft selection easier to follow.

Visual mount labels distinguish representative supports, unverified geometry and placement conflicts. Targeted mounting and thumbnail-residency fixes improve the preview without claiming exact geometry for every product.

Also under validation: audio teardown, terrain-loading lifecycle, window/display behavior and loading guidance. Read the detailed changelog.

Know the limits
before you download.

KESTREL is alpha software. Automated checks and a completed cook do not prove a complete hands-on playtest.

Component data, predicted performance and representative models are aids to simulation. They are not real-world fit, airworthiness or flight-calibration evidence.

Not included yet

  • Full action rebinding across keyboard, gamepad and radio.
  • An OSD editor and rotating multiplayer aircraft cards.
  • Live weather or online address and point-of-interest lookup.
  • Exact models and complete mechanical-fit validation for every catalog item.

Still separate acceptance work

Physical-radio behavior, two-computer LAN, complete camera behavior, updater behavior and end-to-end packaged catalog, display and data workflows. Map binding tests are not proof of hardware input.
