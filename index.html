Early Design-Partner Program KESTREL

Drop a pin anywhere on Earth. Fly the mission before you fly it.

KESTREL loads that exact coordinate in photoreal terrain, flies an aircraft built from the parts your team actually stocks, and scores the run against the mission you built on that ground. Place objectives, OPFOR, and structures where they really are. Fly it in daylight, at dusk, or on the low-light camera after dark.

The clip is not a render. It is ninety seconds of a live session — free flight, the build garage, the mission builder, a night pass on the low-light sensor, and the scored run.

LANTERN — Video to Floorplan

One drone goes in. Everybody gets the floorplan.

LANTERN turns drone or walkthrough video into a georeferenced interior floorplan for ATAK — no survey, no ground truth, about two minutes on a laptop. The assault force, the QRF, and the TOC all see the same interior before anyone else goes through the door.

The image is not a mockup: a palm-sized quadcopter flew one 60-second pass through a building, and LANTERN rebuilt the interior in three dimensions from nothing but its onboard video.

Video in · Floorplan out · ATAK overlay · No LiDAR, no GPS
